Studio

A studio can create a controlled and minimalist environment, offering a clean aesthetic and a polished, professional look. It can serve as a tool for almost any kind of photoshoot, ranging from artistic album covers to professional portraits. I usually prefer to shoot in a studio when working on portraits, promotional materials, acting and modeling portfolios, and clothing showcases.
